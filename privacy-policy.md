# Privacy Policy — Binance Scanner

Last updated: 2026-10-21

Binance Scanner is a personal-use application operated by a single individual
(the repository owner) for their own cryptocurrency market-data analysis. It
is not distributed to or used by the public.

## What data this app accesses

This app requests access to Google Sheets and Google Drive scopes solely to
read and write spreadsheets in a private Google Drive folder owned by the
same individual who operates this app. No data is shared with any third
party, sold, or used for advertising.

## Data stored

The only data written to Google Sheets/Drive is market data (prices, trading
volume, and related statistics) pulled from public Binance API endpoints. No
personal, financial-account, or identifying information is stored.

## Contact

Questions about this policy can be directed to the app owner via the contact
email listed on the associated Google Cloud OAuth consent screen.
